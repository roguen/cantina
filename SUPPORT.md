# Support expectations

Cantina is a personal hobby project built for one specific theater PC. It is public so
the work can be read, not because it promises a product.

- **No support commitment.** Issues and pull requests are welcome and read, but there is
  no response-time promise and no roadmap obligation to anyone else's use case.
- **The deployment target is fixed.** Windows 10 Pro 22H2 with stock YARG 0.15 through
  YARC Launcher. Other configurations may work; nothing is tested against them, and
  issues about them may be closed as out of scope.
- **Security reports** go through the channel in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not the
  public issue tracker.

The project's own records — decisions, roadmap, environment — live in
[`project/`](project/) and are the honest statement of what is and is not done.
